About this library

This library accompanies the two-day course. It is written for engineering managers, team leads, CTOs, VPs of Engineering, and transformation leads working in organisations where AI is accelerating individual and team output — and where that acceleration is creating new coordination demands that existing structures were not designed to handle.

The underlying premise of the course — and of this library — is that AI increases the coordination burden, not just the delivery speed. As output increases, so does the need for alignment, integration, and cross-team decisions. The constraint shifts from individual productivity to how work moves across people and teams. These resources cover the skills, frameworks, and practices that help you address that constraint directly.

Each resource stands alone and can be read before, during, or after the relevant module. Together they cover every major concept, framework, and method introduced in the course.
