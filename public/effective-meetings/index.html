About

About this library

A great deal of everyday leadership work happens inside meetings, and the quality of those meetings depends on a small number of learnable choices, many of them made before the meeting even starts. These resources cover those choices: what a meeting is for, who needs to be there, which format fits, and which simple tools help while it is running.

For moving a group from open discussion to a decision it can commit to, see Facilitating Convergence.
