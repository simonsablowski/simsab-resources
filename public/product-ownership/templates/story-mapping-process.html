Story Mapping Process

Story mapping visualises the user journey, organises work around user value, and plans incremental releases.

This is a preview of the A4 worksheet. Download the PDF to print it, or read Story Mapping for background.

StepWhat you doOutcome
1. FrameDefine the product or feature by answering: What? Who? Why?Shared understanding of the goal and the users.
2. MapIdentify the key user types and their high-level activities (the backbone).An end-to-end user journey.
3. ExploreBreak activities into tasks, and discuss variations, exceptions and alternative paths. Involve different perspectives.Detailed user tasks.
4. SliceGroup stories into incremental releases that each deliver value. Define goals and success measures for each release.A product roadmap with viable releases.
5. PlanDeliver incrementally: walking skeleton, then expand functionality, then polish and refine.A development strategy for continuous delivery.
