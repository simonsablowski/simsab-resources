Kano Model

Sort candidate features into these five categories before prioritising. Invest in performance features and delighters, and deliver basic features without over-investing.

This is a preview of the A4 worksheet. Download the PDF to print it, or read Prioritisation Techniques for background.

CategoryMeaningFeatures
BasicMissing it causes complaints, having it creates no delight.
PerformanceSatisfaction rises in proportion to how much you deliver.
DelightersUnexpected extras that create disproportionate satisfaction.
IndifferentCustomers do not care either way. Usually not worth building.
DissatisfactionActively upsets customers. Avoid.
